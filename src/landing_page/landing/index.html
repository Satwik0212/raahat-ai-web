Not “where's the nearest mechanic” — but “I don't know what help I need or what to do next.”

Maps can find a pin. They can't tell you whether it's an emergency, how serious it is, or what to do right now.

    Road accident + injury

Capabilities

What RAAHAT adds on top of maps.

🧠

Situation-aware decision engine

Classifies what happened and how serious it is before recommending anything — act on understanding, not a pin drop.

English / Hindi, with Hinglish handled at the classification layer. Built on Sarvam STT/TTS.

03

Route-aware OFFLINE safety pack

05

06

Multi-source resilience

Google Places is the primary live source; cached or OSM-derived data can back it up without changing the UI contract.

06
How it works

From panic to a plan, in four.

1

Tell RAAHAT what happened

Type or speak — English, Hindi or Hinglish. One rough sentence is enough.

2

It classifies severity + needs

Incident type, severity and confidence — grounded guidance and exact services.

3

Finds & ranks real services

Live providers, ranked with a stated reason — distance, category, source on every card.

4

You CALL / NAVIGATE / follow guidance

Clear next actions with confirmation for anything safety-critical. You're moving again.
